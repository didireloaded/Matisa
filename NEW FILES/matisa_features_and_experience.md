
# MATISA: FEATURES I WANT AS A USER
# & THE EXPERIENCE I EXPECT

---

## PART 1: FEATURES I WANT AS A USER

### A. DISCOVERY & FIRST IMPRESSION

1. **Guest Browsing**
   - I want to open the app and immediately see content without signing up
   - Let me browse rooms, notes, events, and profiles as a visitor
   - Only ask me to sign up when I try to interact (like, comment, join, message)
   - This removes the signup barrier and lets me "taste" the community first

2. **Live-First Home Screen**
   - Show me what's happening RIGHT NOW, not what happened yesterday
   - Active voice rooms at the top with real listener counts
   - Recent notes from people near me
   - Events happening this week in my city
   - People I might know (mutual connections, shared interests)
   - If nothing is live, show me "trending today" instead of empty sections

3. **Hyper-Local Filtering**
   - Default to my current city (Windhoek, Swakopmund, etc.)
   - Let me switch cities easily to see what's happening elsewhere
   - Show distance from me on profiles and notes ("2km away")
   - Prioritize content from people in my city over random viral content

4. **Smart People Discovery**
   - Don't just show me random people. Show me people I SHOULD know
   - "You both follow @hanna_d" or "3 mutual friends" or "Both into photography"
   - "Recently active near you" — people who were online in the last hour
   - One-tap "Say Hi" with a pre-filled message based on shared interests

### B. CONTENT CREATION

5. **Voice-First Composer**
   - The default action when creating should be voice, not text
   - Hold-to-record with visual waveform feedback
   - Auto-transcribe voice to text (so people can read if they can't listen)
   - Voice notes should feel as easy as sending a WhatsApp voice message
   - Text should always be available as a secondary option

6. **Contextual Creation**
   - If I'm on the Notes tab, the create button makes a note
   - If I'm on Events, the create button makes an event
   - If I'm in a room, the create button starts a new room
   - Don't make me choose from a menu of 6 options every time

7. **Ephemeral Stories That Actually Disappear**
   - 24-hour stories, but make them feel casual, not performative
   - No public view counts (or at least hide them from the creator)
   - Voice stories — record a quick thought, no camera needed
   - Reply to stories with voice or text (private, not public)

### C. VOICE ROOMS & AUDIO

8. **Room Discovery That Makes Sense**
   - Show me rooms by: Live Now, Scheduled Today, Topics I Follow, Friends Inside
   - A room card should tell me: topic, host, how many people, who I know inside
   - "3 friends listening" is more compelling than "142 listeners"
   - Scheduled rooms should let me "Remind Me" (push notification)

9. **Lurker-Friendly Room Experience**
   - 90% of users will listen, not speak. Optimize for them
   - Show my avatar in the audience grid so I feel present
   - One-tap reactions (clap, heart, fire) that make noise for the room
   - "Request to Speak" should be visible but not pushy
   - Hand raise queue that's clear and fair

10. **Karaoke as a Mode, Not a Separate App**
    - Karaoke should live INSIDE voice rooms, not as a separate tab
    - "Start Karaoke Mode" once a room has 10+ people
    - Song queue that's visible to everyone
    - Audience can rate performances (fun, not competitive)
    - Save performances to profile (optional)

### D. MESSAGING

11. **Voice Messages That Feel Natural**
    - WhatsApp-style voice messages in DMs and groups
    - Play voice messages at 1x, 1.5x, 2x speed
    - Waveform scrubbing (jump to any point)
    - Auto-transcribe for accessibility
    - Voice messages should be the default, text secondary

12. **Group Chats That Don't Suck**
    - Named groups with avatars
    - Voice rooms that spawn from group chats ("Let's hop on a call")
    - Event planning inside groups (polls, RSVPs, shared calendar)
    - Pin important messages
    - Reply to specific messages (threaded)

13. **Voicemail for Missed Connections**
    - If someone calls me and I'm offline, they can leave a voice message
    - Voicemail inbox with transcription
    - "Call back" button that starts a voice room invite

### E. EVENTS

14. **Events as Social Glue**
    - Events should feel like invitations, not calendar entries
    - "Who's going?" with friend avatars
    - "Carpool?" feature for events
    - Check-in at the event unlocks a "I was there" badge
    - Post-event photo/video sharing in a dedicated space
    - Events should be createable by anyone, not just verified hosts

15. **Event Discovery by Vibe**
    - Filter by: Today, This Weekend, Free, Music, Tech, Food, etc.
    - "Events your friends are going to" section
    - "Popular in [City]" trending events
    - Reminder notifications 1 day and 1 hour before

### F. PROFILE & IDENTITY

16. **Voice Intro as First Impression**
    - Every profile should have an optional 30-second voice intro
    - Play button right on the profile header
    - "Hear my voice before you message me"
    - This is Matisa's signature feature — lean into it

17. **Availability Status That Means Something**
    - "Available" = open to DMs and room invites
    - "Open to Collaborate" = actively looking for projects/partners
    - "Looking for Work" = job seeker mode
    - "Hiring" = recruiter mode
    - Status should affect discovery (filter people by status)

18. **Skills & Interests That Drive Discovery**
    - Tag-based skills (photography, coding, music, etc.)
    - "People with similar skills" recommendations
    - "I need help with X" posts that match to skilled users
    - Endorsements (lightweight, not LinkedIn-style)

### G. NOTIFICATIONS & ACTIVITY

19. **Notifications That Respect My Time**
    - Batch notifications: "3 people liked your note" instead of 3 separate pings
    - Quiet hours (customizable)
    - Priority notifications only for DMs and room invites
    - "Digest mode" — get a daily summary instead of real-time

20. **Activity Feed as Social Proof**
    - "Hanna and 3 others joined the Afro-pop room"
    - "Jason posted a new note about photography"
    - "Your friend Maria is now hosting a room"
    - This makes the app feel alive even when I'm not in a room

### H. UTILITY & ACCESSIBILITY

21. **Data Saver Mode**
    - Compress images to low-res
    - Don't auto-play videos or voice
    - Stream audio at 16kHz mono
    - Show data usage stats
    - Essential for the Namibian market

22. **Light Mode by Default**
    - Dark mode is beautiful but unusable in sunlight
    - Ship light mode as default
    - Auto-switch based on time of day (optional)
    - High contrast mode for outdoor use

23. **Offline Drafting**
    - Write notes offline, auto-post when connected
    - Queue voice messages for upload
    - View cached content when offline
    - Critical for spotty connectivity

24. **Language Support**
    - English, Afrikaans, Oshiwambo at minimum
    - Auto-detect language in voice notes (future)
    - Translation for notes (future)

## PART 2: THE TYPE OF EXPERIENCE I WANT

### The Feeling: "I Just Walked Into a Party"

When I open Matisa, I want to feel like I just walked into a crowded room where:
- People are laughing and talking in the corner (voice rooms)
- Someone is handing out flyers for tonight's event (events)
- A friend waves me over to introduce me to someone new (people discovery)
- There's a bulletin board with notes and opportunities (notes)
- I can join any conversation or just observe

**This is NOT:**
- Scrolling through an algorithmic video feed alone in my room
- Posting content into the void hoping for likes
- Watching other people's highlight reels
- Feeling like I need to perform for an audience

### The Vibe: Casual, Local, Human

**Casual:**
- No pressure to create perfect content
- Voice notes with background noise are fine
- Notes can be typos and all-caps and emojis
- Rooms can be 3 people or 300 people
- No follower counts on profiles (or at least de-emphasized)

**Local:**
- I see people from MY city, not influencers from Lagos or London
- Events are things I can actually attend
- Notes reference local places, slang, issues
- The app feels like it was built FOR Namibia, not adapted TO Namibia

**Human:**
- I hear people's voices before I see their photos
- Profiles feel like people, not brands
- Conversations happen in real-time, not async comments
- The app facilitates meeting in person, not just chatting online

### The Session Types

**Morning Commute (5-10 min):**
- Scroll through notes from people near me
- Check if any voice rooms are live
- See events happening this week
- Maybe leave a quick voice reply

**Lunch Break (15-20 min):**
- Join a live voice room about a topic I care about
- Listen while eating, maybe raise hand to speak
- Check messages and reply with voice notes
- Browse events and RSVP to one

**Evening Wind-Down (30-60 min):**
- Host or join a karaoke room
- Have longer conversations in voice rooms
- Message friends and plan weekend activities
- Post a note about my day

**Weekend (Variable):**
- Attend an event I found on Matisa
- Meet people in person who I first connected with on the app
- Post photos/voice notes from the event
- Join post-event rooms to recap

### The Success Metric

I know Matisa is working when:
- I open it and there's always something live happening
- I've met 3+ people in person who I first connected with on the app
- I check it before I check Instagram or WhatsApp status
- I feel like I'm part of a community, not just a user of an app
- I can find someone to collaborate with, learn from, or hang out with
- The app feels like it gets me and my city

### The Anti-Patterns (What to Avoid)

**Don't make me feel:**
- Like I'm performing for an audience (TikTok problem)
- Like I'm missing out on something (Instagram problem)
- Like I need to curate a perfect profile (LinkedIn problem)
- Like the app is empty or dead (cold start problem)
- Like I'm talking to bots or fake accounts (trust problem)
- Like my data is being drained (practical problem)

**Don't show me:**
- Content from people I'll never meet
- Viral videos that have nothing to do with my life
- Empty sections with "Create one!" as the only option
- Notifications for things I don't care about
- Ads (at least not in the first year)

## SUMMARY: THE MATISA PROMISE

> **"Open Matisa and find your people."**

That's it. That's the whole app.

Not "Open Matisa and watch videos."
Not "Open Matisa and build your brand."
Not "Open Matisa and scroll forever."

**Find your people.**

People who live near you.
People who share your interests.
People who are doing things you want to be part of.
People who will become friends, collaborators, mentors, or just good conversation.

Everything else — voice rooms, notes, events, karaoke — is just a way to make that connection happen.
